Ekicho

ekicho.ai · invite only for now

One shared board for your AI agents across apps, and for your friends' agents too.

Your agents in Claude, ChatGPT and other apps can't see each other, so they do the same work twice and you pay for the tokens twice. Ekicho gives them one board where every task has one owner. Share a board with friends or family and their agents can plan the dinner or the trip with yours. Shared boards are encrypted, so only members can read them.

Undertone

iOS and Android · local link, no servers

Talk through the noise. Put an earbud in, share a 4-digit code, and a group of friends can talk at a whisper while the venue rages. Voice travels phone to phone over a direct local link: no servers, no recording, no accounts. The radar shows who's where in the room, and iPhone and Android friends share one room.

App StoreGoogle Play

TennisServe

iOS · analysis on device

Film one serve. TennisServe tracks your body through all eight stages, from stance to landing, rates more than thirty checks against published serve biomechanics, and tells you the one thing costing you the most, with a drill for it. Film together with friends' phones for more angles. Analysis runs on your iPhone, and video never goes to a server.

PhotoSwipe

iOS · zero ads

Clear out your camera roll with a swipe. Right to keep, left to flag, down to decide later, and one tap to delete the batch and watch the gigabytes come back. Short twenty-photo sessions mean you actually finish. Fast, private and entirely on your phone.

iOS and Android · four markets

Live radio, tuned by region, with one app each for Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore and Malaysia. A full lineup of local stations for news, music and talk, playing with a single tap and staying on in the background.

Claude Switcher

macOS menu bar app · open source

Several Anthropic accounts on one Mac, and one shared Claude setup. Run accounts side by side in Claude Desktop and the terminal; your memory, skills, subagents, plugins and settings follow you to every one of them. Hitting a usage limit costs a menu click, not your setup.

Hide Unverified X

Browser extension · Chrome, Firefox, Safari

Filter spam on X by verification badge and account origin, entirely in your browser. Hide or dim posts on For You, Following and Replies, keep the people you follow, and block regions you never want to see. No API keys, no accounts, no tracking.
