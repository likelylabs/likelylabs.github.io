Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how Likely Labs Ltd handles information in our app, TennisServe.

Information We Collect

TennisServe does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data. We collect nothing.

Everything Runs on Your Device

Pose estimation, scoring, and drill selection all run on your iPhone. Your videos, the body landmarks the app detects, your scores, and any player names or heights you enter are stored only inside the app on your device. They are removed when you delete the app. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

No Accounts

TennisServe does not require an account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name or email address.

Third-Party Services

TennisServe does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting services. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Camera and Photos

The app uses the camera only when you tap record. When you choose a video from your library, the app uses Apple's photo picker. Only the video you choose reaches the app. The app does not browse or read the rest of your library.

Sharing

You can export a PDF report of a serve. The PDF goes only where you choose to send it.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Likely Labs Ltd does not receive your name, email address, or payment details.

Children

TennisServe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. As described above, the app collects no information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Your continued use of the app after any changes will be regarded as acceptance of the updated policy.
